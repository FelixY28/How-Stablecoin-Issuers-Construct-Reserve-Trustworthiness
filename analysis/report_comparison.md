## Reading notes

- **Boilerplate versus deviation.** The standard, criteria and exclusion paragraphs are fixed template text, identical across Tether's two reports. The analytically interesting lines are where the template changes: the engaging party (added in 2026Q2), the responsible party, and the signing entity.
- **Standard versus criteria.** The standard says how the auditor works (ISAE 3000, AICPA attestation standards). The criteria are the measuring stick, and both issuers write their own. BDO's phrase "the criteria established in ISAE 3000" conflates the two; the wording is unchanged across years.
- **Audit versus attestation.** Neither report is an audit of financial statements. Both give reasonable assurance on one report at one or two dates.

## Comparison questions

**Q1. Claimed versus provided assurance.** See `memo.md`, Finding 3(a). The audit release presents an opinion on the financial position "as of December 31, 2025" (T-PR-20260813-p003) as "the proof of that stability" (p011). The BDO attestations are narrower still: one date, normal market conditions, extraordinary conditions and custodian illiquidity excluded (p.3–4).

**Q2. Earliest versus latest Tether report.** See `memo.md`, Finding 3(b). Same standard, template and exclusions; new engaging party, a different responsible party, a different signing entity.
